Manage your data, tracking preferences, and tool options.

Tracking & Privacy

This tool uses privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics to understand how students use the calculator. No personal information is collected. All calculation data is stored only on your device (browser local storage).

When enabled, anonymous events (such as “GPA calculated” or “result shared”) are sent to our analytics. No names, emails, or IDs are collected. You can turn this off at any time.

Your Data

Your courses, semesters, and preferences are saved in your browser so they persist when you return. This data never leaves your device unless you export it.

About

SZABIST GPA Calculator is an unofficial tool built for SZABIST students. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by SZABIST.

  • Grading scale: Matches the SZABIST undergraduate scale (A+ 90–100 = 4.00 … F 0–54 = 0.00).
  • Data storage: Local browser storage only. Nothing is sent to a server.
  • Version: 2.1.0
