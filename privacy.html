Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information we collect

SZABIST GPA Calculator is a client-side tool. Your course names, credit hours, grades, percentages, and preferences are stored only in your browser's local storage. They are never transmitted to our servers or to any third party.

2. Analytics

We use privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics (Umami) to count page visits and anonymous usage events. No personal identifiers are collected. You can disable extended event tracking in Settings.

3. Advertising

This tool may display advertisements in reserved slots. Advertising partners may receive standard, non-personal signals (such as page referrer or device class) per their own policies. Ad slots are clearly separated from calculator content.

4. Data retention

Your data stays on your device until you clear it (via Settings → Clear all saved data) or clear your browser data.

5. Contact

Questions? Reach out through the repository or the contact link on the homepage.
